# Code of conduct

Cineleaf welcomes people of every background and experience level. Be respectful, patient, specific, and constructive. Discuss the work rather than attacking the person. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Serious conduct concerns can be reported privately through the security contact method in `SECURITY.md`; do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Everyone is encouraged to assume good intent once, acknowledge mistakes, and improve the work together. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid retaliation.
