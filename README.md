## 🖥 Using the software

When you open Cineleaf, you see a workspace. This space contains your preview monitor, your timeline at the bottom, and your file bin on the side. 

### Importing your video files
Drag your video files from your computer folder directly into the file bin. You can also click the Import button to select files through a menu.

### Working on the timeline
Drag a file from the bin into the timeline. Each video clip sits on a track. Use your mouse to drag the ends of these clips to trim them. You can cut clips into parts to remove unwanted sections. 

### Exporting your finished video
When you finish your work, click the Export button. Choose your save location and name your file. The program renders your video based on the settings you choose. This process takes time based on the length of your clip and your computer speed.

## 📁 Managing your project files

Cineleaf relies on a local-first approach. This means the program stores your project data on your hard drive. It does not send your footage to the cloud. You keep full control of your movies. Always save your project folder in a safe place. If you move your video files to a new folder, you must link them again inside the editor. 

## 🛠 Support and troubleshooting

If the program fails to start, restart your computer. Check for Windows updates to ensure your system supports the latest media drivers. Disable other heavy programs while you render your video to give Cineleaf more power.
